% Experimental protocol for the default repeated-CV configuration.
Ten repetitions of five-fold cross-validation are stratified by fault class and split complete recordings before excerpt sampling. Each split provides 800 training recordings and 200 test recordings. Nested labelled subsets with $k\in\{1,2,5,20\}$ episodes per class are shared across methods, and held-out recordings enter neither pretraining nor supervised fitting. Each method uses one training initialization per split and label budget, with final checkpoints evaluated. The twelve-method comparison therefore includes 2,400 fitted classifiers.

At $k=1$, three nested random pool selections per split vary the total number of available pretraining recordings over $N_U\in\{1,2,5,10,20,50,100,200,400\}$ without class balancing. The two proposed methods share the pools and fixed labelled subsets. Since each recording contains one hour of observations, $H_U=N_U\,\mathrm{h}$ measures available history, while the full pool contains 800 hours. The reduced pools add 2,700 classifier fits, and full-pool endpoints reuse matching classifiers from the main comparison.

Robustness is measured at $s\in\{0,0.1,0.2,0.3\}$, using three shared perturbation realizations per positive severity. Macro-F1 is averaged over realizations and pool selections where applicable, followed by the five folds within each repetition. Reported means and sample standard deviations summarize the ten repetition means and do not assume that overlapping folds are independent.
